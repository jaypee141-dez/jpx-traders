Frequently asked questions

Got Questions?

Quick answers to the most common questions.

Is JPX Traders safe and secure?

Your journal is only visible to your account. Passwords are hashed, sessions use secure cookies, JPX never asks for your broker password, and you can export or delete your data at any time.

Which platforms are supported (MT4/MT5)?

Connect any MT4 or MT5 account with your broker server, login and password and your trades import automatically. Other brokers and markets can be logged manually.

How does copy trading work?

Pick a trader on the leaderboard and choose the account to copy into. Their trades are mirrored into your journal in proportion to your balance, or at a fixed multiplier. Copy trading carries risk; you stay in control and can pause or stop at any time.

Can I use multiple accounts?

Yes. Pro supports up to 5 accounts and Elite up to 25. Track live accounts, demos and prop-firm challenges separately or together.

What payment methods do you accept?

Plans are billed by card through our payment provider. Every new account starts with a 14-day Pro trial and no card is needed to begin.

Is JPX Traders financial advice?

No. JPX Traders is a journaling and copy-trading tool. Leaders are independent traders, not advisers. Trading involves risk and past results do not predict future results.
