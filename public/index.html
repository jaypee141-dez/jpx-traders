From broker history to a clear review in three steps

1. Bring trades in

Live sync from MetaTrader 5 with an Expert Advisor, a CSV import from MT4 or MT5, or log trades by hand for any market.

2. Add context

Tag the setup, rate your execution, record the amount risked and what you were feeling. Takes seconds per trade.

3. Read the numbers

Profit factor, expectancy, drawdown, R-multiples and breakdowns by hour, weekday, symbol and setup, all computed from your own trades.

Analytics that answer questions

Which setup actually makes money? Do you lose more on Fridays? Is your edge in longs or shorts? Every chart is filtered by account and date range and reads straight from your trades.

  • Equity curve and drawdown
  • P&L by weekday, hour, symbol, setup and tag
  • R-multiple distribution, streaks, payoff ratio

A calendar of your results

See every trading day at a glance, with weekly totals. Open a day to review its trades and jump straight into that day's journal entry.

Journal and discipline

Write a plan before the session and a review after it. Set daily loss, trade count and losing-streak limits and see today's progress against them as you trade.

Built for MetaTrader users

Each account gets its own API key. The JPX Sync Expert Advisor sends closed positions with exact UTC times, commission and swap. Multiple accounts and prop-firm challenges are tracked separately.

Your data stays yours

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, sessions use HttpOnly cookies, and you can export every trade as CSV or delete your account whenever you like.
